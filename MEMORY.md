## Product: AdSpector
- AdSpector (adspector.ai) is bro’s product.
- Positioning: Google Ads + Merchant Center optimization platform and **command center**.
  - Dashboard shows what’s burning budget (waste) and lets you exclude waste (search terms / products) directly in the interface via Google Ads API (no digging for IDs).
  - Also includes audits, alerts, AI Advisor, and feed management.
- Key modules/capabilities (canonical details in `docs/adspector_capabilities.md`):
  - Dashboard: account health score, wasted search terms, top/worst products, feed health; one-click negatives + product exclusions.
  - Audits: structural + performance + AI semantic alignment + change history; export reports.
  - Alerts: conversions/spend anomalies, PMax channel mix, MC eligibility changes, URL health; Slack integration.
  - AI Advisor: uses live Ads data; proposes actions with approval; can generate Search/PMax campaigns; EN+DE.
  - Feed management: optimize Merchant Center feed (rules, buckets, title builder, AI suggestions, COGS/profit analysis, image generation, sheets export/sync).
