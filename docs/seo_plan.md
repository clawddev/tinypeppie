# SEO Plan (EN) — AdSpector

Goal: grow organic traffic + qualify agency and e-commerce intent while staying aligned with AdSpector’s capabilities.

## ICP context
- Primary buyers: DE agencies managing multiple Google Ads accounts.
- Content language: English (blog), with potential DE localization later.

## Positioning to weave in (soft)
AdSpector = Google Ads + Merchant Center optimization platform / command center:
- Dashboard shows waste fast + in-app actions
- Audits + Alerts
- AI Advisor (campaign building)
- Feed Management (rules, title builder, buckets)

## Content strategy
### Pillar clusters (initial)
1) Google Shopping Feed Optimization
2) Performance Max (PMax) optimization & control
3) Search terms, negatives, and waste reduction
4) Google Ads account audits & structure
5) Merchant Center diagnostics (eligibility, disapprovals, URL health)

### Funnel mapping
- Top-of-funnel: how-to guides (titles, PMax, negatives)
- Mid-funnel: audit checklists, templates, workflows for agencies
- Bottom-of-funnel: comparisons and “best tools for X” (careful, credible)

## Internal linking rules
Every article should include:
- 1 link to /features
- 1–2 links to the most relevant AdSpector capability page/section
- 1 link to a practical next step (demo / trial / contact) if you have it

## Publishing cadence (starter)
- 2 articles/week for 4 weeks (8 total) → then evaluate in Search Console.

## Output format for the SEO/Blog Writer agent
For each article create:
- `outputs/seo_en/outlines/<slug>.md`
- `outputs/seo_en/drafts/<slug>.md`
Including: meta title + meta description + slug + internal link suggestions.
