# Content Brief — Merchant Center Disapprovals (Fixes + Prevention)

## Primary keyword
merchant center disapprovals

## Secondary keywords (variants)
- google merchant center disapproved products
- shopping ads disapprovals
- merchant center account suspension vs disapproval
- product disapproved limited
- item disapproved fix
- merchant center policy violations
- landing page not working merchant center

## Search intent
Informational / troubleshooting. Audience: e-commerce marketers + agencies.

## Goal
Help readers:
- identify the *type* of disapproval (item-level vs account-level)
- fix the most common causes quickly
- prevent recurring disapprovals with a monitoring checklist

## Key promise
Most disapprovals are fixable fast once you categorize them (data issues, policy issues, landing page issues, shipping/tax settings, mismatched pricing/availability).

## H1 suggestion
Merchant Center Disapprovals: Causes, Fixes, and Prevention (2026)

## Outline (H2/H3)
### H2: Disapproval vs Suspension vs Limited (important definitions)
- Item disapproved
- Item limited
- Account issues/suspension

### H2: The fastest way to diagnose the root cause
- Start from Diagnostics
- Read the exact issue + affected attributes
- Check “affected countries” + channel

### H2: Common disapproval categories (with fixes)
#### H3: Feed/data quality issues
- missing GTIN/brand
- invalid/missing attributes (price, availability, condition)
- invalid identifiers

#### H3: Price & availability mismatches
- landing page price mismatch
- sale price mismatch
- availability mismatch

#### H3: Shipping & returns settings
- missing shipping info
- unrealistic shipping times/costs

#### H3: Landing page / URL problems
- 404/redirect loops
- blocked by robots.txt
- slow/timeouts
- mobile issues

#### H3: Policy violations
- restricted products
- unsupported claims
- promotions policy

### H2: Step-by-step fix workflow (agency SOP)
- isolate one SKU
- fix attribute or page
- reprocess feed
- request review if needed

### H2: Prevention checklist
- change log
- scheduled URL checks
- feed health monitoring

### H2: Soft product note (optional)
Mention alerts for eligibility changes + URL health + feed rules/change history.

## Examples required
