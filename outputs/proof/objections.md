# Objections & replies (DE agencies)

## 1) “Why not just do this in Google Ads?”
**Reply:** You *can*, but the time sink is finding the right place (campaign/ad group/listing groups) and repeating it across accounts. AdSpector surfaces waste in one dashboard and lets you execute exclusions immediately — less context switching, fewer missed leaks.

## 2) “Is it safe to let a tool change our account?”
**Reply:** Actions are explicit and controlled. The AI Advisor proposes changes and requires approval before execution. For dashboard actions, you decide exactly what gets excluded.

## 3) “We already have scripts / rules.”
**Reply:** Scripts are great, but they’re hard to audit and explain to clients. AdSpector combines actions with visibility, change history, and exportable reports — easier to operationalize across a team.
